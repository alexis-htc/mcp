# Simple Pricing, Deep Infrastructure

We have different pricing models depending on the model used. Some of our langauge models offer per token pricing. Most other models are billed for inference execution time. With this pricing model, you only pay for what you use. There are no long-term contracts or upfront costs, and you can easily scale up and down as your business needs change.

#### [Service Tiers](https://deepinfra.com/pricing\#service-tiers)

Choose how each inference request is scheduled. The standard tier is the default; the priority tier schedules your requests ahead of standard traffic for faster time-to-first-token during peak demand, at a higher per-token price.

| Tier | Scheduling | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | Default scheduling, best-effort during peak demand | 1x base price |
| Priority | Scheduled ahead of standard traffic for faster time-to-first-token | 1.5x base price |

Enable per request by setting `service_tier: "priority"`. Availability varies by model — check the model page. [Read more](https://docs.deepinfra.com/chat/overview#service-tier).
